# Final Project Report (EDUC 244 only)

- Due No Due Date
- Points 70

The goal of this final project assignment is to present original work in applying machine learning paradigms from class to a dataset with a compelling real-world application. While the machine learning competition was designed to sharpen your model optimization skills, this project should have a broader scope in which you explain the real-world context that could benefit from your machine learning approach. Think back to the introduction lecture where we discussed who the stakeholder or consumer would be for each scenario.

The final project must be completed in groups of 3 to 7 members. You may mix-and-match between DATA 144 and EDUC 244 students, but only EDUC 244 students will be responsible for contributing to the 244-only Final Project Paper assignment.

This final project report should summarize your project's work in the format of an academic report. This assignment is only for students enrolled in EDUC 244, the graduate version of the course.

## Paper Details

The report consists of group and individual portions. The report should be single-spaced, 12pt font, in the following format:

- **Introduction (group, ~1 page):** Describe your research question(s) and discuss the project context and motivation (who are the stakeholder(s) and what are the benefits to them?)
- **Dataset (group, ~1 page):** Describe the raw dataset: where or how did you collect it, descriptives, feature engineering, filtering, etc.
- **Methods (group, ~1 page):** Describe each team member's role in the project, introducing the work that will be described in detail in the individual sections to follow
- **Individual Work (1-2 pages per individual):** Describe the work that you, as an individual, contributed to the project. Make sure to discuss your contributions in detail, noting how you applied machine learning concepts and techniques. Please include your name on each page of your individual sections.
- **Results and Conclusions (group, ~1 page):** Synthesize the group's work and provide findings and conclusions. Please include a specific sub-section on <u>Real-World Implications</u> of your project.
