# Final Project Presentation

- Due No Due Date
- Points 25
- Available after Nov 4 at 2:30pm

The goal of this final project assignment is to present original work in applying machine learning paradigms from class to a dataset with a compelling real-world application. While the machine learning competition was designed to sharpen your model optimization skills, this project should have a broader scope in which you explain the real-world context that could benefit from your machine learning approach. Think back to the introduction lecture where we discussed who the stakeholder or consumer would be for each scenario.

The final project must be completed in groups of 3 to 7 members. You may mix-and-match between DATA 144 and EDUC 244 students, but only EDUC 244 students will be responsible for contributing to the 244-only Final Project Paper assignment. Any groups with more than 7 members will incur a 20% penalty per person. Any groups with fewer members will not be permitted to present due to a lack of available presentation spots. The effort expected from each group member is approximately equivalent to the effort required for two labs. Consequently, there will be higher expectations for larger groups.

## Presentation Details

The following are suggested sections of an effective presentation.

- **Introduction:** Describe your research question(s) and discuss the project context and motivation (who are the stakeholder(s) and what are the benefits to them?)
- **Dataset:** Describe the raw dataset: where or how did you collect it, descriptives, feature engineering, filtering, etc.
- **Methods:** Describe the machine learning methods you used to approach the problem at hand. You may use algorithms that we did not cover in class, but please give some background info if you do.
- **Results and Conclusions:** Synthesize individual work and provide findings and conclusions. Do not forget to mention the *implications* of your project and its results. Would you say it's ready to be used in the real-world yet? If not, what needs to be solved before you would say it was ready?

## Expectations

Every member is expected to present for at least two minutes. Every group, regardless of the number of members, should present for 15 minutes plus 3 minutes for questions. All group members will receive the same grade for the final presentation. While the same rubric will be used to assess both DATA 144 and EDUC 244 students, the presentation will count for 100% of the DATA 144's final project grade and 30% of the EDUC 244's final project grade (the other 70% being the paper).

Due to the size of the class and limited days to present, smaller groups will not be allowed. You may use a private dataset so long as that dataset can be shared with your group members. It is not sufficient for your final project to simply copy a Kaggle competition prediction task.
